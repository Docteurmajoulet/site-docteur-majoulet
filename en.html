Eye surgeon in west Paris, consultations in English

Dr Alexandre Majoulet · Retina and cataract surgery · Boulogne-Billancourt (92100)

Dr Alexandre Majoulet is an ophthalmic surgeon specialising in vitreoretinal surgery. He sees patients in English and in French at the Cabinet Ophtalife, 37 rue d’Aguesseau, in Boulogne-Billancourt, on the western edge of Paris, and operates at the Clinique Jouvenet (Paris 16th). He also works at the Quinze-Vingts National Eye Hospital (Paris 12th), in Professor Baudouin’s department.

He trained at the Paris university hospitals (residency 2017–2022), then held a post-residency hospital post at the Quinze-Vingts (assistant des Hôpitaux de Paris, 2022–2024). He holds the French specialist diploma in ophthalmology (2023), a university diploma in vitreoretinal surgery (Université Paris-Saclay, 2024), and is a Fellow of the European Board of Ophthalmology (FEBO, 2022). French medical registration number (RPPS): 10101500121.

The rest of this website is in French. This page gives the essentials in English.

What we treat

  • Retinal tears, treated by laser at the practice, and retinal detachment, treated by surgery: scleral buckling or vitrectomy (French page on retinal detachment).
  • Macular hole and epiretinal membrane: vitrectomy (macular hole, epiretinal membrane).
  • Age-related macular degeneration (AMD): diagnosis with OCT on site and intravitreal injections in a dedicated ISO 7 room at the practice (AMD, injections).
  • Diabetic retinopathy and retinal vein occlusion.
  • Cataract surgery and lens implants (French page on cataract surgery).
  • Floaters and flashes, glaucoma and dry eye.

Eye emergencies

  • A dark curtain or shadow over the vision of one eye, or a sudden drop in vision: the eye must be examined the same day. During opening hours, call the practice on +33 1 84 19 11 66. Outside opening hours, go directly to a 24-hour eye emergency department: Quinze-Vingts (Paris 12th, +33 1 40 02 16 80), Fondation Rothschild (Paris 19th, +33 1 48 03 68 84) or Hôpital Cochin (Paris 14th, +33 1 58 41 23 00).
  • Suspected central retinal artery occlusion (sudden, painless and severe drop in vision in one eye): during opening hours, come to the practice urgently; otherwise, go directly to the Fondation Rothschild.
  • Call 15 (emergency medical service) for an eye injury, a chemical splash or a loss of vision in both eyes, and when a drop in vision comes with difficulty speaking, weakness of an arm or a leg, or a drooping face.
  • New flashes of light or new floaters, without a curtain: an eye examination within 24–48 hours. The practice keeps urgent retinal appointments for this.
  • Straight lines that suddenly look wavy or distorted: an examination within 24–48 hours.
